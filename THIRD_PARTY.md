## Frontend dependencies

Locked versions and transitive packages are recorded in `package-lock.json`. React, Vite, TypeScript, viem, qrcode, WalletConnect and their dependencies retain their upstream licenses. WalletConnect UI chunks are generated by its library's dynamic imports but its hosted UI/analytics are not initialized: the application uses `showQrModal: false`, `telemetryEnabled: false`, and a local pairing QR. License notices extracted from bundled dependency packages are shipped in `public/dependency-licenses.txt` / `dist/dependency-licenses.txt`.
